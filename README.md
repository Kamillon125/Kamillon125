### about (me!)

I study how people acquire languages through interactive media, using NLP and corpus linguistics to measure comprehensible input.

- 🎓 **Studies:** MSc in Machine Learning & AI @ AGH University of Krakow
- 📜 **Background:** B.A. in Social Informatics, Game Studies research club, and quantitative CAWI surveys
- 💬 **Focus:** Second Language Acquisition (SLA), specifically in interactive media, and even more specifically in video games
- ❄️ **Desktop:** Big fan of tiling window managers and unconventional workflows - currently running *swayfx* on *NixOS*
- ⌨️ **Typing:** I peaked around *~200 WPM* (with a 10 word PB of 300), which is completely and entirely useless in practice because my brain is way slower than 200 WPM

### featured work

- 🎮 **[the_language_of_video_games](https://github.com/Kamillon125/the_language_of_video_games)**  
  NLP pipeline measuring dialogue complexity across 56 games using spaCy & fine-tuned DeBERTa (CEFR levels). *2nd place in Applied CS @ "Hutnicza" Students' Conference 2026.*
- 📊 **Language Acquisition in Gaming (Pilot Study)**  
  Empirical paper evaluating Krashen’s Affective Filter hypothesis via non-parametric analysis (Wilcoxon). *AGH University Press (2026).*
